A jam maker ships blueberry jam from two kitchens to three shops. Kitchen K1 can supply
up to 40 crates and kitchen K2 up to 50 crates. Shop S1 needs 30 crates, shop S2 needs
25 crates and shop S3 needs 20 crates. The cost of shipping one crate is: from K1, 4 to
S1, 6 to S2 and 9 to S3; from K2, 5 to S1, 3 to S2 and 7 to S3. Crates are shipped whole.
Find the shipping plan that meets every shop's need at the lowest total cost.
